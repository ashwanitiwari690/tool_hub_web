Our Privacy Approach: Browser-Based Execution

We believe that small utilities should respect your data. Whenever technically feasible, our tools run entirely within your browser's execution environment using client-side JavaScript and the HTML5 Canvas API.

Your text, code, passwords, and photos are processed in your local device memory. They are not uploaded to our servers, stored in a central database, or analyzed for advertising profiles. For detailed information on our privacy standards, please review our Privacy Policy.
