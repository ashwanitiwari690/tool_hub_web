Percentage Calculator covers the three most common percentage questions: finding a percentage of a number, finding what percentage one number is of another, and calculating the percentage change between two values.

% of a number: (percentage ÷ 100) × number

X is what % of Y: (X ÷ Y) × 100

Percentage change: ((new − original) ÷ |original|) × 100
