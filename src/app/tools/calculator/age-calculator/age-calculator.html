Chronological age represents the precise measure of time that has elapsed from an individual's birth to the present date. Unlike simple approximations that merely subtract birth year from the current year, true chronological age computes the exact combination of completed calendar years, completed months, and remaining days, accounting for leap years and irregular month lengths.

Calendar arithmetic is complex because months in the Gregorian calendar span 28, 29, 30, or 31 days, and leap years occur every four years (with century exceptions). The ToolNova Age Calculator implements date subtraction that borrows days from the preceding month whenever the current day is less than the birth day, guaranteeing mathematical and calendar accuracy.
