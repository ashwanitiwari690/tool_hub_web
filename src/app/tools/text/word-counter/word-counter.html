ToolNova Word Counter is a free, instant text analysis utility that measures the length, density, and typographical structure of your writing in real time. As you type or paste content into the editor, the tool calculates total words, total characters, characters excluding spaces, sentence counts, paragraph divisions, and estimated silent reading duration.

Whether you are an author polishing a chapter, a student adhering to strict college essay limits, a copywriter drafting Google Ads headlines, or a social media manager crafting posts for character-constrained feeds, this utility provides immediate, reliable feedback.

Our Word Counter evaluates your text using client-side JavaScript algorithms executed directly in your browser's runtime:

  • Word Counting: Text is trimmed and split by Unicode whitespace boundaries (/\s+/). Consecutive spaces, tabs, and line breaks are treated as single separators, ensuring empty lines or double spaces never artificially inflate your word count.
  • Character Counting: Evaluates total string length including all letters, digits, punctuation, spaces, and line feeds. The "no spaces" metric strips whitespace characters (/\s/g) to report raw textual payload.
  • Sentence & Paragraph Detection: Sentences are identified by standard terminal punctuation marks (periods, exclamation marks, and question marks) followed by whitespace. Paragraphs are detected via double newline sequences (/\n\s*\n/).
  • Reading Time Calculation: Estimated using the widely accepted average adult reading speed benchmark of 225 words per minute.

  • Hyphenated Words: Compound terms like "well-known" or "state-of-the-art" are counted as single words according to standard typographic rules.
  • Numbers & Symbols: Standalone numbers (e.g., "2026") and currency values (e.g., "$100") are counted as separate words when separated by spaces.
  • Multilingual Considerations: Languages without whitespace word delimiters (such as Japanese or Chinese) require specialized character-based parsing. For those scripts, the "Characters (all)" metric provides the most accurate measure.
  • Pasting from PDF or Word Processors: Formatted documents often contain invisible carriage returns (\r\n) or non-breaking spaces ( ). If paragraph counts seem inflated, check for extraneous blank lines between paragraphs.
  • Accidental Trailing Spaces: Trailing spaces at the end of paragraphs do not alter word counts due to automated whitespace trimming, but they do increase the gross character count.
